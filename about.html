About Andrew

Salinas is home.

Born and raised in Salinas, Andrew Sandoval serves the community that shaped him — and the same community where he and his wife are raising their family.

A family story built on work, perseverance, and entrepreneurship.

Andrew’s parents began as field workers before building businesses of their own. They sold bicycles at the original Sky View Drive-In Flea Market, and his father later became a Mayordomo, or crew supervisor, before becoming a business owner on Alisal Street.

His mother became a realtor, broker, and businesswoman. Together, his parents opened Supurb Water Stores and Saul’s Menswear, a longtime East Alisal business.

That example shaped Andrew’s belief in hard work, respect for people, and the responsibility to serve the community that gave his family opportunity.
